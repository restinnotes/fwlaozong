题目: A meta-analysis of periodic and aperiodic electrophysiological features in Parkinson's disease.
年份: 2026  类型: review-article; Review; Journal Article
摘要: Parkinson's disease is characterized by a range of motor and non-motor changes that can negatively impact quality of life. Many studies have identified potential clinical electrophysiological correlates of Parkinson's disease with an aim of developing new methods of identifying at-risk patients and forming the basis of therapeutic interventions. However, these studies do not present consistent results, and a formal meta-analysis is warranted to identify reliable EEG or magnetoencephalography (M/EEG) characteristics across datasets. In this meta-analysis of open-access M/EEG datasets (n = 6; 4 EEG and 2 MEG), we compared periodic and aperiodic characteristics of resting-state recordings in 368 patients with Parkinson's disease and 570 age-matched healthy controls. Specifically, we compared the power and peak frequency of unadjusted and aperiodic-adjusted alpha- and beta-band oscillations, and the exponent and offset, across the two groups. Parkinson's patients had a consistently elevated aperiodic exponent and offset. Patients also had higher unadjusted beta-band power, but this was no longer present when the aperiodic features were removed, suggesting that previous findings of elevated beta activity in Parkinson's disease could be confounded by aperiodic activity rather than reflecting true oscillatory differences. Patients also had higher adjusted and unadjusted alpha-band power and a slower alpha peak frequency compared with controls, whereas no group difference in beta peak frequency was identified. In conclusion, this large cohort meta-analysis points to a broadly consistent pattern of both periodic and aperiodic changes in Parkinson's patients in M/EEG signals that may be used to develop diagnostics and targeted interventions in the future.

摘要: It is currently unclear whether anxiety, cognitive load, or their combination modulates freezing of gait differently and whether people with Parkinson's disease and freezing of gait (freezers) suffer from stronger threat responsiveness than nonfreezing groups. 题目: Time to Move Upstream? A Framework for Expanding the Focus of Freezing of Gait to Prodromal, Subclinical, and Pre-FOG Stages.
年份: 2026  类型: review-article; Review; Journal Article
摘要: Freezing of gait (FOG) is one of the most disabling motor symptoms of Parkinson's disease, yet a fundamental question remains unresolved: why does FOG occur at any given moment? Most research has focused on studying FOG once it is already manifest. The present work aims to synthesize recent observations and propose a conceptual framework in which FOG reflects a dynamic process unfolding across multiple stages and time scales. Subtle changes in gait dynamics and physiology can precede episodes by several seconds (Stage -1), and experimental paradigms show that individuals without clinically recognized freezing may exhibit brief episodes under sufficiently sensitive conditions (Stage -2). Reframing FOG as a multistage process that includes an upstream phase raises new questions regarding mechanisms, classification, and intervention, and may, ultimately, inform strategies aimed at predicting and preventing freezing rather than simply reacting to it.
