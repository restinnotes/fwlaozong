题目: Functional Connectivity Differences of the Subthalamic Nucleus Related to Parkinson's Disease.
年份: 2016  类型: Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't; research-article; Multicenter Study; Journal Article
摘要: A typical feature of Parkinson's disease (PD) is pathological activity in the subthalamic nucleus (STN). Here, we tested whether in patients with PD under dopaminergic treatment functional connectivity of the STN differs from healthy controls (HC) and whether some brain regions show (anti-) correlations between functional connectivity with STN and motor symptoms. Moreover, uncoupling between insula and STN might also induce an insufficient setting of thresholds for the discrimination between relevant and irrelevant salient environmental stimuli, explaining observations of disturbed response control in PD. In sum, motor symptoms in PD are associated with a reduced coupling between STN and a key region for executive function.

题目: Movement disorders induced by deep brain stimulation.
年份: 2016  类型: Review; Journal Article
摘要: Deep brain stimulation represents a major advance in the treatment of several types of movement disorders.
